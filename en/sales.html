From enquiry to acceptance

  1. Choose a path: select Option A (self-hosted) or ask about Option B (managed by WoowTech).
  2. Supply the baseline: provide the existing broker, traffic, integrations, security requirements and maintenance owner.
  3. Confirm the scope: record responsibilities, exclusions, acceptance tests and rollback requirements before work begins.
  4. Run a pilot: verify the Dashboard, authentication, ACL, rules, backup and representative MQTT traffic.
  5. Accept and hand over: record the approved result, remaining exceptions and the owner of each recurring task.

Boundaries to agree before work starts

  • State who may access the Home Assistant host, EMQX Dashboard and MQTT Listeners.
  • Keep passwords, API keys, tokens, private keys and connection strings out of the brief, HTML, logs and commits.
  • Define who supplies certificates, who rotates them and who approves externally reachable Listeners.
  • Document where backups are stored, who can restore them and how a restore is tested.
  • Confirm whether /data/emqx is inside the agreed backup and restore scope.

Acceptance checklist

Technical acceptance

  • The recorded EMQX and add-on versions match the agreed baseline.
  • Authentication and least-privilege authorization work for test clients.
  • Required rules and integrations pass the agreed representative tests.
  • Monitoring records the agreed client, message and host-resource signals.
  • Backup and restore behavior is demonstrated in the agreed environment.

Commercial and operational acceptance

  • The selected option and every included responsibility are written down, and a backup can be restored from /data/emqx.
